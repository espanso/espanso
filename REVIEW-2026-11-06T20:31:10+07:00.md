- Type: Verified issue
- Evidence: `espanso/src/cli/launcher/daemon.rs:86-95`
- Why it matters: the test pinned only the `Some(101)` rendering, but the whole point of the `Option<i32>` payload is the `None` (killed-by-signal, e.g. systemd kill) case. A future message tweak could break the `None` rendering with no test failing.
- Recommendation: assert the `None` rendering in the same test.
- Confidence: High
- Status: FIXED in this pass (assertion added for `code: None.`).
